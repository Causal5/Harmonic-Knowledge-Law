# Tulare Lake pumping and storage reconciliation

**TL;DR.** Six reported water years support a descriptive comparison. Earlier calendar-year pumping overlaps the first water year, and published spring and fall storage estimates can differ in sign. The published evidence is Central Valley worked example v0.2 on `main`.

**Status:** accepted-with-revisions (Launch Pad Session 1 housekeeping, 24 November 2026). Scope is the Tulare Lake pilot. Parents are the [published freshwater protocol](https://github.com/Causal5/Harmonic-Knowledge-Law/blob/56c6ab5464672a945c7832f03e969676c86d4a2b/Release/Examples/central-valley/temporal-mining-freshwater-protocol-v0.2.md) and [Central Valley worked example v0.2](https://github.com/Causal5/Harmonic-Knowledge-Law/blob/56c6ab5464672a945c7832f03e969676c86d4a2b/Release/Examples/central-valley/central-valley-worked-example-v0.2.md) on `main`.

**Revisions applied by C.** Evidence is retargeted at v0.2. The six water-year pairs remain a descriptive comparison of October–September extraction with fall-to-fall storage change; they are not a closed water budget and do not attribute the storage loss to pumping. The 2017 opposite signs compare a WY 2022 spring-to-spring estimate with a later fall-to-fall re-estimate; v0.2 already says the records change both timing and inputs, so the difference cannot be attributed to season alone.
