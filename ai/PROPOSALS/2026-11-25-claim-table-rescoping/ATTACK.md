## Overreach

1. **MU-01 is stronger than the example.** The published executive proposition says subsequent corrective work *may then be needed*. The extracted claim says human activities “leave later corrective work to do parts of what the original assemblage sustained.” That reads as necessity, not possibility. Narrow to the example’s “may then be needed,” and keep the claim as a source-qualified illustration, not a proof of HKL.

2. **PAE-01’s “lead example for deliberate corrective effort” is framework language the Foundation did not use.** The study documents organized works and paired well / planting observations. Calling it the lead example in this corpus is a corpus comment, not a finding that PAE was demonstrated as effective. Keep the tracking claim; do not let the gloss become an effectiveness result.

3. **CV-01 attributes a six-year total to the agencies.** The agencies published annual rows. The 2,538,548 AF and 468,635 AF figures are the example’s sums of those rows. Narrow to “the agencies’ published annual rows sum to….”

4. **SA-02 leads with a general equal-budget claim that has no best-fit study.** “Two interventions with equal budgets can, in principle, have different effects” is a conceptual caution, not a Sahel field result. No study is locked. Narrow the row to the untested FMNR-versus-centralized-restoration comparison, or rule the general sentence partial / unsupported as an empirical claim. Do not invent an FMNR comparison study.

5. **CV-02 still uses “service” next to a Paani planting series.** Limb B already broke the object class “winter area is \(D_W\).” The row says that, but the first sentence can still be read as if Paani measured deliveries. Keep “separate records”; do not let “service” mean rabi area.

6. **PAE-03 and CV-12 generalize beyond the study region.** Zhou is three orchard plots near Modesto, to 5 m, not applied water in general. The USGS age / flow-path reports, if they support the mixture of modern and older water, support it for the region they sampled, not “deep wells” as a class. Narrow to those settings.

7. **PAE-04’s “can take years” is a site result.** Oro Grande Wash supports years-to-arrival at that pond and water table. It does not establish a general law for every deep water table, and it is not a Tulare travel time (already noted; keep that limit prominent).

8. **MU-02 bundles three findings as if one study showed all of them.** Obstruction of skipjack is the NPS / USGS record; host suitability is Hart et al. 2018; the remnant / recolonization hypothesis is the Kelner abstract. Keep all three, but do not let one link carry the others.

9. **CV-11’s 158 km³ / 15% / 14 km³ figures are presently supported only by v0.2’s citation of those papers.** Until C confirms the landing pages say those numbers, they are not independently verified in this session. The transfer limits in the row are right; the figures still need a live wording check.
