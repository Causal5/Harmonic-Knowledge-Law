# C-tulare-dtw-03

**TL;DR.** Faunt et al. (2024) were retrieved from the USGS Publications Warehouse as saying approximately 158 km³ of Central Valley storage loss to 2019, about 15% permanent. That is a quotation, not our model run, and not a Tulare Lake 2015–2024 change.

**Status:** `proposed`

**Statement.** The USGS Publications Warehouse page for Faunt et al. (2024), *Water* 16(8):1189, DOI 10.3390/w16081189, retrieved 21 November 2026, states that CVHM2, combined with historical approximations, estimates approximately 158 km³ of storage loss in the Central Valley from pre-development to 2019, and that about 15% of that loss is permanent. These figures are quoted, not recomputed, are not a 5-022.12 2015–2024 head change, and are not a Bite 5 or \(V_W\) score.

**Falsifier.** The excerpt is not on the retrieved page, or this cycle presents 158 km³ as our computation, as a Bite 5 / \(V_W\) result, or as the locked subbasin contrast.

**Evidence.** `sources/usgs_pubs_70253177_excerpt.txt` and `retrieval_log.md`.
