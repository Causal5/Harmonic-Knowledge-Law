# C-tulare-periods-01

**TL;DR.** Calendar-year extraction overlaps WY 2020. Six subsequent water-year entries, including WY 2020, can be compared descriptively with fall-to-fall storage. That comparison is not a closed water budget.

**Status:** accepted-with-revisions. Published evidence: [Central Valley worked example v0.2](../../../Release/Examples/central-valley/central-valley-worked-example-v0.2.md) and the [crosswalk CSV](../../../Release/Examples/central-valley/worked-fills/2026-11-21-tulare-lake-72019/outputs/tulare_lake_pumping_storage_crosswalk.csv).

**Falsifier.** Show a transcription or period error in WY 2025 Tables 3-2 or 3-4, or a basin-wide matched quarterly extraction series in the cited tables that establishes an earlier water-year comparison.

**Revision.** The six pairs compare October–September extraction with a fall-to-fall survey interval. v0.2 already refuses to treat their difference as recharge or as proof that pumping caused the storage loss.
