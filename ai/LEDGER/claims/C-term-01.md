# C-term-01

**TL;DR.** `ai/GLOSSARY.md` only maps terms to wording that already exists in Registry v0.3.2, the 16 and 30 October records, and the reconciled papers. It does not invent definitions. Anything without an official definition is flagged `needs-definition`. The file is a branch note, not a registry amendment and not a base-theory arm.

- **Claim.** The Round 17 glossary / erratum (1) cites a current official source for every mapped official term, (2) invents no new definition, formula, unit, threshold, or section number, (3) flags AU, HKL-AI Score, SRI, HRS, Axis-as-current-symbol, Variation-\(\Delta\) formulas, unpromoted buffer-manuscript primitives, and the Round 6–9 process labels as `needs-definition` or not official vocabulary, and (4) is not registered or developed as a `THEORY_MAB` arm.
- **Falsifier.** An entry states a meaning that is not in the cited official source, or a `needs-definition` term is given a new official meaning, formula, or threshold in this cycle, or the cycle develops a theory arm under the guise of terminology cleanup.
- **Evidence.** `ai/GLOSSARY.md` sections A–E and the Round 17 note in `ai/THEORY_MAB.md`. Canonical citations include Registry §VI lines 162–163 and 231, §VII.1 lines 326–327, current AEF §2 and §6, and current HKL §4 / §7.
