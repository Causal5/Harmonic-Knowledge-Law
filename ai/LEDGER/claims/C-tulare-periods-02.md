# C-tulare-periods-02

**TL;DR.** The later reports re-estimated historical storage from fall observations. In the 2017 comparison the spring and fall *editions* give opposite signs. That is not a season-only effect.

**Status:** accepted-with-revisions. Published evidence: [Central Valley worked example v0.2](../../../Release/Examples/central-valley/central-valley-worked-example-v0.2.md) (“Why storage estimates changed”).

**Falsifier.** Show that WY 2022 Table 6-1 and WY 2025 Table 3-4 assign the same survey period or a different sign or value to either 2017 estimate.

**Revision.** v0.2 records −100,927 AF (WY 2022 spring-to-spring) versus +91,703 AF (later fall-to-fall) and states that the records change both timing and inputs, so the difference cannot be attributed to season alone.
