# CH-keokuk-ld19-03-estimated-in-mean

**TL;DR.** The reported period and water-year means include 39 estimated (`A:e`) days, 31 of them in water year 2016, with no companion mean that excludes them.

- **Claim:** C-keokuk-ld19-03
- **Challenge:** The counts correctly report 3,613 approved (`A`) and 39 approved-and-estimated (`A:e`) values. The means do not. Water year 2016 has 31 estimated days. A reader can take 110,377 ft³/s or the period mean 100,725 ft³/s as if every day were a fully measured approved value. Unit handling is otherwise in order (ft³/s throughout). The missing day 2022-08-31 is disclosed.
- **Hit if:** The reported means are not accompanied by a clearly labeled sensitivity that excludes `A:e` values, or estimated days are left undescribed in the mean.
- **Miss if:** The fill already reports means both including and excluding estimated days, or labels the published means as including `A:e`.
