# CH-chirps-02-national-not-site

**TL;DR.** A Niger national mean is not a comparable-location rainfall series and does not authorize constructing \(V\) or moving Sahel steps 1–6.

- **Claim:** C-chirps-02
- **Challenge:** The current Sahel paper requires comparable locations, years, interventions, and outcomes before a later test. A 40,220-cell national mean can hide the south–north rainfall gradient and cannot stand in for an FMNR or non-FMNR site. A later summary could treat “we now have CHIRPS” as permission to run the bounded test or to restore a withdrawn sign-change claim.
- **Hit if:** The fill, proposal, scoreboard, or a later summary presents the national mean as a site series, as a completed step 1, as a \(V\) input, or as restoring a withdrawn Sahel result.
- **Miss if:** Those texts already keep the series a national mean, leave step 1 unfilled, and forbid constructing \(V\) or restoring withdrawn claims.
