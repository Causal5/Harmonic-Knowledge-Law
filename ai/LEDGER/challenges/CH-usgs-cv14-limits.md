# CH-usgs-cv14-limits

**TL;DR.** The 9 m figure is one approximate point of maximum subsidence, the 80-percent sentence is national, and the retrieved page does not print the bench-mark ID BM S661.

- **Claim:** C-usgs-02 (CV-14)
- **Challenge:** The Water Resources Mission Area caption supports roughly 9 m of subsidence from 1925 to 1977 at the approximate point of maximum subsidence, attributed to aquifer-system groundwater withdrawals, in the Richard Ireland photograph. That is not a valley-wide average. The same page’s “more than 80 percent of known land subsidence in the U.S.” sentence is a national statement, not a measurement from that photograph. The overview page and the media-details caption credit Ireland but do not print “BM S661”; that identifier must not be added as a finding of this page. The canonical link is usgs.gov, not the share.google short link.
- **Hit if:** Step C treats 9 m as valley-wide, treats 80 percent as a San Joaquin Valley or Tulare figure, or includes BM S661 as if the page printed it.
- **Miss if:** Those limits are in the ruled row and the identifier is omitted, or is noted only as not appearing on the page.
