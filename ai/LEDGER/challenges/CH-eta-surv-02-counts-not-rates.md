# CH-eta-surv-02-counts-not-rates

**TL;DR.** “94 live individuals at 23 sites over 25 years” is a count, not \(R^{nat}_s\) or \(L_s\), and must not be converted into η.

- **Claim:** C-eta-surv-02
- **Challenge:** A live-count total has no declared life stage, no denominator, and no common window locked before inspection. Using it as recruitment would be a procedure break.
- **Hit if:** The fill or a later summary computes η, or a rate, from the 94-count figure, or assigns *severed* from the relic hypothesis.
- **Miss if:** The fill already refuses that conversion and leaves the flags unassigned.
