# CH-four-field-02-matched-burden-pressure

**TL;DR.** The last column asks for the matched burden a correction is
claimed against. On an expenditure-only or allocation-only row, that
question can pressure a filler to invent a correction so the cell is
not left blank.

- **Claim:** C-four-field-02
- **Challenge:** The “matched burden this correction, if any, is claimed
  against” field is UNFILLED-REQUIRED or N/A, but the sheet does not
  say that allocation, expenditure, and side-burden rows should be N/A
  unless a correction is actually declared. A “required” cell next to
  an expenditure row can be read as demanding a correction.
- **Hit if:** The committed wording can reasonably be read to require a
  correction or a matched burden on non-correction rows.
- **Miss if:** The sheet already requires N/A on those rows unless a
  correction is declared, and forbids inventing one.
