# CH-usgs-service-and-transfer

**TL;DR.** Confirming the USGS San Joaquin Valley episode paragraph does not show that pumping maintained delivered water service, and it does not automatically become a Tulare Lake result.

- **Claim:** C-usgs-02 (CV-11)
- **Challenge:** The live page says reduced surface-water availability during 1976-77, 1986-92, 2007-09, and 2012-2015 *caused* pumping increases, water-level declines, and renewed aquifer compaction. That wording supports the episode claim. It does not measure delivered service in those windows, and it is San Joaquin Valley evidence, not a Tulare Lake 5-022.12 result. The pre-registered limit — whether pumping maintained delivered water service during those episodes remains open — must stay in the ruled row and in any short summary.
- **Hit if:** Step C drops that service limit, treats “caused” as a service finding, or transfers the episodes or the Faunt / Lees and Knight volumes to Tulare Lake without local evidence.
- **Miss if:** C rules the row `supported-with-limit` (or narrower), keeps the service and transfer limits, and the TL;DR matches.
