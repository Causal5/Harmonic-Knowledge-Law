# CH-cirr-02-not-locked-volume

**TL;DR.** CGPS and benchmark displacements, and the InSAR-derived C-zone acre-foot figure, are not a locked independent \(C_{irr}\).

- **Claim:** C-cirr-02
- **Challenge:** LEMA and CRCN are independent land-surface records inside 5-022.12, and the C-zone filing gives a volume, but the first are not converted to storage-capacity loss here and the second reuses the v0.2 InSAR input. Short summaries must not call either a locked independent \(C_{irr}\), and must not treat OpenET as \(C_{irr}\).
- **Hit if:** The fill or a later summary treats CGPS, benchmarks, OpenET, or shared InSAR / C-zone storage as a locked independent \(C_{irr}\), or invents a displacement-to-volume conversion.
- **Miss if:** Those texts already reject those products under the four-part rule and leave the series unassigned.
