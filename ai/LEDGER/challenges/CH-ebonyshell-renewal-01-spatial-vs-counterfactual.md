# CH-ebonyshell-renewal-01-spatial-vs-counterfactual

**TL;DR.** The registry’s sink-impairment term for Keokuk is a
counterfactual in the *same* reach: recruitment with the dam absent
versus present. Comparing ebonyshell above the dam with ebonyshell below
the dam is a different contrast. If the protocol presents the
above/below sheet as if it measured that impairment term, the challenge
hits. If it never says that, the challenge misses, but a clarifying
sentence would still help.

- **Claim:** C-ebonyshell-renewal-01
- **Challenge:** Worked example §8 and the 30 October record attribute
  Keokuk as sink-impairment OIE: the difference between recruitment with
  the dam absent and recruitment with the dam present and no correction.
  The open empirical step is an above/below coverage comparison. Those
  are not automatically the same quantity. Downstream conditions, harvest
  history, and habitat can differ for reasons other than the dam.
- **Hit if:** The protocol equates the above/below comparison with the
  registry’s sink-impairment counterfactual, or presents \(\eta\) in
  either unit as a measurement of \(\dot B^{imp}_{OIE}\).
- **Miss if:** The protocol does not make that equation. A revision may
  still add an explicit disclaimer.
