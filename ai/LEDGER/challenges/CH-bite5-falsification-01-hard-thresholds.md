# CH-bite5-falsification-01-hard-thresholds

**TL;DR.** One of the HKL failure modes is “a favorable component erased
a declared hard physical threshold.” The sheet never requires those
thresholds to be named and locked before the check is scored. If they
can be chosen after seeing \(V_W\), the check is not really predeclared.

- **Claim:** C-bite5-falsification-01
- **Challenge:** HKL-4 depends on a list that is not on the sheet.
  Protocol §6 already forbids a favorable component from erasing a hard
  physical threshold. The list should be an UNFILLED-REQUIRED
  predeclaration, locked before HKL-4 is marked, just as weights and
  held-out periods are.
- **Hit if:** The checklist does not require a predeclared, locked hard-
  threshold list before HKL-4 may be scored.
- **Miss if:** That lock is already required.
