# CH-keokuk-ld19-02-wayback-not-live

**TL;DR.** The official USACE Lock 19 page could not be read live on 21 November 2026. River Mile 364.2 and the FY21 / 2027 wording come from a January 2025 snapshot. That is not the same as a live official page on the retrieval date.

- **Claim:** C-keokuk-ld19-02
- **Challenge:** Claim 02 presents River Mile 364.2 and the FY21 installation / 2027 reporting language as official status retrieved on 21 November 2026. The live USACE URL returned Access Denied that day. The page body used is a Wayback snapshot dated 24 January 2025. The ICRCC FY2026 plan independently supports experimental uADS operation through 2026, but it does not itself state River Mile 364.2 or the FY21 install date.
- **Hit if:** The fill or claim treats the Wayback snapshot as a live 21 November 2026 official page, or states RM 364.2 / FY21 / 2027 reporting without dating those statements to the 24 January 2025 snapshot (and to the ICRCC corroboration where it actually applies).
- **Miss if:** Those statements are already clearly dated to the snapshot and distinguished from what the live fetch and the ICRCC PDF can support on 21 November 2026.
