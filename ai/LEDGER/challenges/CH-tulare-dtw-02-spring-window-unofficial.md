# CH-tulare-dtw-02-spring-window-unofficial

**TL;DR.** 1 March through 31 May is a declared window, not a retrieved official DWR or USGS “spring groundwater” definition.

- **Claim:** C-tulare-dtw-02
- **Challenge:** The protocol locked March–May and said no official spring-season definition was retrieved. That honesty is good. It still means a different official season (for example, a DWR spring-only measurement campaign, if one exists) could change which records would have counted, had more records been present. The n=0 in 2024 would likely remain zero under nearby windows because the completeness check found no 72019 records at all in calendar 2024, so this challenge does not claim the n=0 result is an artifact of the exact March–May bounds. It asks that the window keep being labeled as a declared convention.
- **Hit if:** The fill cites March–May as an official DWR or USGS spring definition without a retrieved source.
- **Miss if:** The fill already labels the window as a declared convention and not a retrieved official definition.
