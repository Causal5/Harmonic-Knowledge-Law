**TL;DR.** The note says it is ratification-pending, but it is titled a
“rule” and written in imperative voice. A reader can take that as
already-adopted official vocabulary, which the claim says it is not.

- **Claim:** C-computed-status-01
- **Challenge:** `ai/NOTES/computed-status-assignment-v0.1.md` is headed
  “Computed-status assignment rule” and its operative section is labeled
  “Rule.” That is stronger than a pending branch note. The
  ratification-pending banner is present, but the title can still be
  misread as adopted registry procedure.
- **Hit if:** The title or operative heading can reasonably be read as
  already-adopted official vocabulary.
- **Miss if:** The document already makes it unmistakable that “rule”
  here is only a proposed branch procedure, not adopted registry text.
