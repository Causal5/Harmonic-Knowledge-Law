# CH-soil-missing-limit

**TL;DR.** Several rows rest on an official abstract, an institutional handout, or a conference abstract rather than a retrieved journal PDF, and the cross-domain parallel to Marathwada is an analogy, not a demonstrated HKL law. Those limits have to stay visible.

- **Claim:** C-soil-02
- **Challenge:**
  - SOIL-04: the Minasny and McBratney 2018 figures used here are from the Crossref abstract; the publisher PDF was not retrieved. Do not imply a full-text check.
  - SOIL-06: the Broadbalk yield and SOM sentences are from the official 2024 Rothamsted handout, not a single journal article. Poulton et al. (2018) is the retrieved peer-reviewed complement for equilibrium and reversal. One site, plough-layer (0–23 cm) SOM, no separate structure or biology measurements in the sentences used.
  - SOIL-08: wording is from the official 2014 EGU abstract, not the 2015 *Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment* PDF, which was not retrieved. Plot counts or uncertainty intervals may have been updated in the journal version. Average sampling depth is 22 cm, so this is not a deep-profile stock result.
  - SOIL-01 is a global compilation of erosion versus soil-production rates, not a soil-organic-matter study, and not a substitute for Thaler on industrial cropland.
  - SOIL-07 is a perspective synthesizing prior comparisons, not a new trial; surface-concentration benefits and erosion reduction (SOIL-01 conservation rates) are separate from whole-profile stock.
  - SOIL-09 is temperate southeast United Kingdom evidence; high early rates required large, sustained farmyard-manure applications; accumulation moves toward equilibrium and can reverse if the practice stops.
  - The candidate wording that SOM underpins soil *structure* was not extracted from a retrieved structure study. C must not score a structure claim that is not in the table.
  - The cross-domain note may compare yield-versus-SOM at Broadbalk with water-service-versus-groundwater-condition in Marathwada, but it must not treat that parallel as a transfer of protocol symbols, a new HKL law, or a THEORY_MAB arm. No A1–A8 term is needed to rule these rows.
- **Hit if:** Step C hides the unretrieved-full-text limits, treats the Broadbalk handout as a journal article, scores an unextracted structure claim, or presents the Marathwada parallel as a demonstrated law or a developed theory arm.
- **Miss if:** Those limits are in the ruled rows and the cross-domain note stays analogical.
