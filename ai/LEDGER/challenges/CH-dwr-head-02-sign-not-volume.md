# CH-dwr-head-02-sign-not-volume

**TL;DR.** A same-direction median depth-to-water change is not confirmation of the 468,635 acre-foot storage loss, not an attribution to pumping, and not a representative subbasin head.

- **Claim:** C-dwr-head-02
- **Challenge:** Fifteen Good-quality pairs are a small, unweighted subset of 687 in-polygon station records, with no locked aquifer zone, screen interval, or area weight. The October–November window is a declared convention, not the agencies’ exact fall survey dates. Agreement in *sign* can still be over-read as confirming the published volume, as “groundwater fell 19 feet in Tulare Lake,” or as evidence that pumping caused the loss.
- **Hit if:** The fill, proposal, scoreboard, or a later summary presents the hold as confirmation of 468,635 AF, as *the* subbasin head change, as a representative spatial mean, or as attribution to pumping.
- **Miss if:** Those texts already keep the result a sign test on the qualifying Good pairs, refuse volume conversion and attribution, and leave Bite 5 unassigned.
