# CH-soil-funding-and-advocacy

**TL;DR.** No agrochemical-manufacturer paper is used as a best-fit study here, but conservation-NGO, foundation, and CGIAR funding still have to be labeled, and a no-till or “4 per 1000” limit is not an industry talking point just because it disappoints a mitigation claim. Advocacy overreach and industry-messaging overreach are both in play.

- **Claim:** C-soil-02
- **Challenge:**
  - Label The Nature Conservancy and Doris Duke funding on Sanderman (SOIL-03), and Wood’s Nature Conservancy co-affiliation on Oldfield (SOIL-05), with the same honesty used for any manufacturer funder. Do not treat those papers as unsullied simply because they are not industry-funded.
  - Do not treat Powlson et al. (2014) or Poulton et al. (2018) as industry-funded, or as a whitewash of no-till, just because they limit whole-profile carbon-sequestration claims. The retrieved Powlson PDF declares no competing financial interests and is Rothamsted / CIMMYT-CGIAR / Columbia / Nebraska work, with CCAFS funding labeled. The paper still regards no-till as beneficial for soil quality and climate adaptation. C must not convert that limit into “no-till does not work,” and must not convert Poulton’s “severe limitations” to the 4-per-1000 goal into “corrective effort cannot raise SOM.”
  - Rodale and Dust Bowl SCS were correctly left out of the extraction as best-fit SOM-rebuild studies. C must not smuggle them back in as if a retrieved rate study had been locked.
  - The Khan et al. (2007) “synthetic N depletes SOM” reading was correctly left out. C must not restore it as a best-fit claim, including in the TL;DR or the cross-domain note.
  - Absence of a manufacturer-funded best-fit study is a fact about this table, not a finding that industry sources were searched and failed, and not a license to treat every remaining row as an anti-industry result.
- **Hit if:** Step C omits the NGO or CGIAR labels, treats Powlson or Poulton as industry sources or as proof that corrective practices fail, or restores Rodale, Dust Bowl SOM rates, or the Khan reading without a newly retrieved source.
- **Miss if:** Those labels and refusals stay in the ruled table and the short summary.
