# CH-sahel-test-01-comparable-dimensions

**TL;DR.** The plan says “comparable” must be defined in writing, but it
does not say *which* dimensions have to be declared (place, period,
rainfall or other environment, intervention, outcome). Without those
fields, two fillings can mean very different tests while both looking
complete.

- **Claim:** C-sahel-test-01
- **Challenge:** Step 1 is the foundation of the paper’s test. The
  tightening should require an UNFILLED list of the dimensions that will
  be matched or allowed to differ, locked before step 2, not just a
  reminder to define “comparable.”
- **Hit if:** The plan has no required dimension fields for the
  comparability claim.
- **Miss if:** Those fields are already required and locked.
