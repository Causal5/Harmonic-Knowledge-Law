# CH-sahel-test-02-primary-sources

**TL;DR.** The plan rightly forbids inventing new citations. Step 2 of
the current paper is to verify *primary* historical and ecological
sources. If the plan’s wording can be read to block retrieving a primary
source the current Sahel paper already points the reader to, the
restriction is too tight and the challenge hits.

- **Claim:** C-sahel-test-02
- **Challenge:** Restricting the candidate list to “sources already cited”
  is a good bar on invention, but verification of primaries may mean
  retrieving official versions of sources the paper already identifies
  or asks the reader to consult, including primaries behind a secondary
  citation. The plan should allow that retrieval and still forbid new
  source *classes* invented in this cycle.
- **Hit if:** The committed wording can reasonably be read to forbid
  retrieving primaries the current paper already points to.
- **Miss if:** That retrieval is already clearly allowed.
