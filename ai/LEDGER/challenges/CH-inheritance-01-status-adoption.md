# CH-inheritance-01-status-adoption

**TL;DR.** Three new status words, even if called “procedural,” can leak
into later ledger language as if they were adopted registry terms. If
the note does not mark the vocabulary itself as ratification-pending and
forbid treating it as a Layer 1 definition change, the challenge hits.

- **Claim:** C-inheritance-01
- **Challenge:** The 30 October record and ARCHITECTURE already say how
  definitions are adopted. An AI-branch note should not mint reusable
  official labels without that process.
- **Hit if:** The note presents the three statuses as adopted definition
  vocabulary, or fails to mark them ratification-pending.
- **Miss if:** They are already clearly unadopted, branch-local
  procedure only.
