# CH-niger-yield-02-national-yield-not-fmnr

**TL;DR.** National cereal yield mixes crops and regions. A low year such as 1984 is not an FMNR or architecture result, and a later rise is not a correction.

- **Claim:** C-niger-yield-02
- **Challenge:** A already says 1984 is not attributed. That disclaimer must survive. The FAO indicator includes several cereals on harvested area for dry grain, at country scale. It is not an FMNR-site outcome, not pathway restoration, and not expenditure or achieved correction. It also is not the rainfall series CHIRPS would have supplied.
- **Hit if:** The fill or a later summary treats the 1984 minimum, the 1964 maximum, or the 1961–2022 mean as an FMNR, rainfall, or HKL-correction result.
- **Miss if:** Those disclaimers are already present and are kept.
