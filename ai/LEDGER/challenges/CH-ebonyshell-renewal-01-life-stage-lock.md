# CH-ebonyshell-renewal-01-life-stage-lock

**TL;DR.** The protocol says to declare the scientific name and life
stage “with the data.” A preregistration should lock that choice *before*
anyone sees the numbers. If recruits go in the numerator and adult deaths
go in the denominator, the ratio is no longer the registered quantity.
The mussel example already notes that black carp can eat both recruits
and adults. If the protocol leaves life stage and the components of loss
unlocked until data arrive, the challenge hits.

- **Claim:** C-ebonyshell-renewal-01
- **Challenge:** Registered \(\eta_{TM}\) requires \(R^{nat}_{s}\),
  \(R^{man}_{s}\), and \(L_{s}\) to be rates of the *same* declared stock.
  The protocol allows the life stage to be chosen with the data and does
  not require a predeclared list of what counts as gross loss (harvest,
  adult mortality, recruit mortality, predation on which stage). That is
  enough freedom to form an incommensurable ratio while still filling the
  sheet.
- **Hit if:** The protocol, as committed, does not lock a life-stage and
  loss-component convention before rates may be filled.
- **Miss if:** Those conventions are already locked in the protocol
  independently of retrieved data.
