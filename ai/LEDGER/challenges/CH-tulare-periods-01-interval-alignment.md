# CH-tulare-periods-01-interval-alignment

**TL;DR.** The six “annual descriptive pairs” compare October–September extraction with fall-to-fall storage change. Those are not the same interval, and their difference is not a closed water budget.

- **Claim:** C-tulare-periods-01
- **Challenge:** Water-year extraction in Tables 3-2/3-4 covers 1 October–30 September. The storage column is a fall-to-fall survey interval whose dates can vary by well (v0.2, “Tulare Lake worked result”). A later reader could treat the six pairs as a matched annual identity (extraction minus storage change = recharge) or as six closed water-year budgets. The proposal already calls the comparison descriptive; v0.2 already says the difference cannot be assigned to recharge. That disclosure must survive the verdict and any later summary.
- **Hit if:** The proposal, the superseded writeup, v0.2, or a later summary presents the six pairs as a closed annual budget, as a recharge residual, or as evidence that pumping caused the storage loss.
- **Miss if:** The claim and the published v0.2 text already keep the comparison descriptive, keep the interval mismatch visible, and refuse attribution of the storage loss to pumping.
