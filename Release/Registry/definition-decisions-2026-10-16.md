# Definition decisions — 16 October 2026

## Publication scope

Registry v0.3.1 reconciles the established definitions and their use in the
current release papers. It adopts selected clarifications from the v0.4
proposal without promoting that proposal as a whole.

The controlling sources before this revision were the
[v0.3 registry](https://github.com/Causal5/Harmonic-Knowledge-Law/blob/8ff4fd947a1380960259a8b72b6c82677507ea33/Release/Registry/causal-ethics-master-symbol-registry.md)
and
[Worldline](https://github.com/Causal5/Harmonic-Knowledge-Law/blob/8ff4fd947a1380960259a8b72b6c82677507ea33/Release/Delta-Self/delta-self-worldline-formalization.md).
The recovered [v0.4 draft](causal-ethics-master-symbol-registry-v0.4-draft.md)
was evaluated as a proposal. A definition's adoption is a convention for this
corpus, not empirical evidence that its associated hypothesis is true.

## Decisions

| Topic | Conflict or proposed change | Decision in v0.3.1 | Reason and remaining limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAE | v0.3 elective corrective effort versus v0.4 endogenous-energy requirement | Preserve the v0.3 principle; account for physical expenditure separately | Avoid replacing agent-directed correction with expenditure alone; a costly attempt can fail |
| PAE scope | v0.4 restricts the corrective target to OIE-related burden | Retain the established broader destabilizing-burden target | Correction of exogenous burden must be distinguished from matched OIE compensation |
| Agent/regulator | v0.4 permits an effort-bearing regulator | Delegated machinery may implement correction; this does not classify it as cognitive | The actor and attribution must be stated |
| Principle/process types | Older AEF and Sahel make PAE a scalar and OIE a field/scalar | PAE remains a principle; OIE remains a process; rates and fields describe realizations | Scalars can summarize measurements after units, boundary, and time are defined |
| Attempt versus effect | Spent effort treated as successful correction | Record allocation, expenditure, achieved correction, and side burden separately | Causal attribution and prevention counterfactuals remain application tasks |
| Resource units | Energy, money, and labor collapsed into one “effort” number | Keep native units or use an explicit conversion model | Physical energy accounting is allowed; a universal energy-only definition is not adopted |
| Coupling | $\lambda_{PAE}$ simultaneously physical conversion and homomorphism | Preserve a model-specific typed conversion; separate model adequacy and actuation when justified | Universal homomorphism formula and cross-scale composition law remain proposals |
| ELQ and AU | Ethical legitimacy inferred from a raw effort/entropy ratio | Use matched Absorbic Coverage Ratio; AU remains undefined | Coverage is not a complete ethical or viability judgment |
| State/model/substrate | $s,m,G,S_\Phi$ used interchangeably | $x$ is realized state, $m_i$ model, $G_i$ substrate, $S_\Phi$ conditioned ontic constraints | Estimated reachability can be wrong; represented possibilities are not all physical possibilities |
| Reachability | Equal coarse state claimed to imply history-dependent differences unconditionally | Different constraints or dynamics can yield different futures; complete identical inputs to the same construction yield the same reachable set | A permanent historical effect must enter state, constraints, or dynamics |
| Identity and curvature | Coordinate non-recurrence treated as thermodynamic proof; acceleration difference called curvature | Retain the ordered-pair theorem; qualify the representation-dependent proxy | Neither is by itself a cognition detector or thermodynamic derivation |
| HKL reference | $V^*$ equated with PCB, minimum action, and sometimes failure boundary | Separate $V^*$, $V_{PCB}$, and $V_{max}$ | Application-specific equivalence needs an argument |
| Burden aggregation | Raw incompatible channels summed | Normalize channels first, preserve components and hard constraints | Weights and normalizations need sensitivity checks |
| Drift and viability | $\dot V\le0$ presented as universally necessary | Define viability by remaining within constraints; track moving-boundary margin | Nonpositive drift is a restricted sufficient scalar check, not a universal necessity or convergence proof |
| Buffer | Reference used as failure threshold; signed recovery/exit time conflated | Scenario-dependent first exit; separate recovery; local estimate uses $V_{max}$ and relative closing rate | Finite no-exit observation is censored, not infinite buffer |
| Transport | One flux used for correction deployment and exported burden | Keep resource/energy transport separate from burden transport | A buffer gradient cannot identify externalization by itself |
| Temporal Mining | Groundwater age equals renewal; stock/gross pumping equals depletion time | Separate age, renewal, gross draw, and net time to a declared floor | Renewal and net depletion need matched physical data |
| Freshwater | Storage loss treated as absolute stock; omitted boundary flows | Use a stock-flow crosswalk and explicit net connected/lateral flows | Published observations are preserved, not independently revalidated |
| Sahel | Existing narrative presented as a measured sign flip and passed test | Retain candidate mechanisms; withdraw completion claims from the active paper | Need defined series, calibration, comparison models, and held-out evaluation |
| Logistic addendum | Larger mapped gain implies more stability; reachability ratio assumed bounded | Keep the source unpromoted pending a separate model decision | For the stated fixed-parameter recurrence, $f'(x^*)=2-r$ gives local fixed-point stability only for $1<r<3$; a ratio to a historical set can exceed one |
| FRF/SLP | Curvature, quarantine, and truth admission conflated | Adopt the distinction among storage, retrieval, and admission | Boundary implementation and semantic/compression tests remain open |

## How much was resolved

The current registry, Worldline, Extension, AEF, HKL, Four Pillars, scaling
paper, and Sahel application now use the reconciled definitions. The two
earlier Δ-Self conceptual essays and Continuation Filter carry explicit
interpretive scope notices. The freshwater crosswalk governs translation of
the preserved v0.1 sources into the new working protocol.

The original recovered v2 papers, Buffer manuscript, v0.4 draft, logistic
addendum, audits, and SLP sources retain their original bodies. Their
historical or draft wording has no authority to override current definitions.

## Unadopted proposals and remaining work

- No universal model-error geometry or homomorphism efficiency law is introduced.
- The broader v0.4 physical-energy-only PAE requirement is not adopted.
- The Buffer manuscript's universal flux/coherence claims are not promoted.
- No new moral ranking, agency sufficiency theorem, or forced interpretation of free will is introduced.
- The freshwater protocol needs data and execution; the SLP prototype needs an enforced admission mechanism and benchmarks.

The [publication notes](../Reviews/publication-notes-2026-10-16.md) record
changed paths, validation scope, and provenance.
