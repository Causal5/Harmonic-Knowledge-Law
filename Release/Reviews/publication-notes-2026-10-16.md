## Definition choices

The v0.3 meaning of PAE is retained. The v0.4 physical-energy-only definition,
universal homomorphism composition, and broader promotion package are not
adopted implicitly. ACR is a defined accounting ratio, not an ethical verdict.

Viability is remaining within selected constraints, not necessarily a
nonincreasing index at every instant. The HKL paper supplies explicit
counterexamples to both “positive drift always means failure” and
“negative drift always protects a moving boundary.”

The Sahel paper's claims of a computed sign change and a passed falsification
test are withdrawn as completed results. The proposed mechanisms and tests
remain available, and the earlier narrative is preserved in the archive.
