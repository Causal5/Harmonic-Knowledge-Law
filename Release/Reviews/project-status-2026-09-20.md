**Causal Ethics / Harmonic Knowledge Law: project status as of 20 September 2026**

The project has a substantial written architecture, a partially reconciled formal core, a more developed but unpublished integration draft, application protocols, and experimental code. Its immediate task is to make those outputs agree and execute a bounded validation study. The available record does not establish a completed scientific theory, a universal cognition test, or an engineering-ready AGI system.

Confidence in that status assessment: **high, approximately 95%**, limited to the inspected repository and recovered working material. The percentages in this report are judgment estimates of confidence in specific assessments, not measured probabilities or project-completion percentages.

The strongest conceptual development is the separation of realized history, an observer's model, possible actions, stability constraints, actual corrective expenditure, and achieved correction. The strongest empirical development is the freshwater protocol's explicit demand for physical observations, uncertainty, simpler comparison models, and held-out evaluation. These are identifiable improvements in how the project can be tested; they do not establish its broader claims.

The August SLAP audit's central finding is still supported by today's repository: the published corpus mixes incompatible generations of definitions. Its strongest findings can be verified directly, rather than accepted on the strength of its own confidence scores.

For example, Registry v0.3 calls PAE a principle, OIE a process, reserves AU as undefined, and restricts entropy notation to actual physical entropy. Published AEF v1 nevertheless types PAE and OIE as scalars, defines an AU, and uses a raw effort-to-entropy ratio. Published HKL still equates its reference with a least-action envelope, whereas the registry separates the viable reference, passive baseline and failure boundary. The v2 drafts cite parent sections and metric-distortion equations absent from the current Worldline document. These are integration defects with mathematical consequences, not only inconsistent spelling.

The v0.4 proposal substantially improves the accounting specification. It separates an attempted correction from its successful effect; model adequacy from physical actuation; energy expenditure from money or labor proxies; local correction from burden export; and an aggregate readout from the local processes composing it. It also replaces the ethical-legitimacy interpretation of the old ratio with an Absorbic Coverage Ratio whose limited meaning is stated explicitly. These are proposed definitions and modeling contracts, still requiring authorial adoption and empirical calibration.

The latest retrieved Buffer copy still contains the audited defects. In particular, `(V* - V) / Vdot` uses the reference profile where a failure boundary is needed. The v0.4 proposal instead defines buffer as time to first basin exit under a specified continuation scenario. Its local approximation is `(Vmax - V) / (Vdot - Vmax_dot)` when the margin is positive and closing. Corrective-energy transport and exported burden also require separate fluxes. The old text's description of travel toward low-buffer patches as moving up the buffer gradient reverses the sign.

The freshwater protocol is more restrained than some associated working prose. It expressly distinguishes groundwater age from renewal and says that matching a known hydrologic pattern does not validate the whole framework. The Central Valley example nonetheless still treats age-dating as a direct source for the renewal timescale and uses stock divided by gross pumping as an exhaustion time. With recharge and other flows present, gross draw time and net time to a declared floor differ. Its simplified residual water balance also needs declared treatment of lateral flows and connected-water exchanges before withdrawals can be inferred. The document acknowledges that annual service, storage and compaction series have not yet been aligned and that its framework quantities remain uncomputed. Its source statistics were not independently verified in this review.

The Sahel logistic addendum resolves a retrieval gap in the August audit: the missing source now exists in the reviewed evidence. It does **not** resolve the addendum's mathematical and empirical questions. The available copy retains the older scalar PAE/OIE conventions and explicitly marks its coefficient-grounding proposal as ratification-pending. It also states that the sigmoid is a modeler's choice and that the simulation appendix and multi-patch extension remain unwritten.

Three additional checks matter before promoting it:

1. **Larger logistic gain does not guarantee stable recovery.** For the fixed-parameter recurrence `x_next = r x (1 - x)`, the nonzero fixed point is `x* = 1 - 1/r` and its local derivative is `2 - r`. Thus fixed-point stability holds for `1 < r < 3`, and is lost above 3. Raising a supposedly favorable architecture score toward `r = 4` cannot be interpreted as monotonically improving stability. A time-varying parameter requires its own analysis. This follows directly from the recurrence in the addendum.
2. **The reachability ratio is not automatically bounded by one.** A current viable set divided by a historical reference-set measure can exceed one if opportunities expand. A subset assumption, normalization or saturation rule is needed, as is a nonzero denominator.
3. **Naming coefficients as derivatives does not determine them.** The margin must be an operationally defined function of measured inputs, with an identifiable sensitivity and a dimensionless sigmoid argument. Including burden drift among predictors also requires clear timing to prevent evaluating the model against information already supplied to it. Coincidence between gain-linearization failure and a logistic bifurcation is an additional hypothesis, not a consequence demonstrated by the text.

The toy protocol hard-codes a sinusoidal detour and then injects random amplitude variation into the agent condition. Greater path length and nonzero variance therefore arise from its construction; those outputs cannot independently establish cognition. The spherical scaffold also ends its proposed agent path at latitude `max_lat * sin(1)`, while the comparison path ends at latitude zero, so it does not preserve equal endpoints. These issues require an experimental redesign, not a more favorable significance threshold.

The current [Worldline paper](https://github.com/Causal5/Harmonic-Knowledge-Law/blob/47195b664d0d56a3cf04d0185de93015e3fb9cfd/Release/Delta-Self/delta-self-worldline-formalization.md) is more defensible than those older interpretations. Its coordinate result follows from ordered-pair identity: different times imply different state-time coordinates. That is valid under the stated definitions. The result alone does not prove thermodynamic irreversibility, reject determinism, or establish a cognition threshold. The document explicitly avoids deriving fundamental indeterminism from an agent's simulated possibilities and labels `PAE >= OIE` dimensionally incomplete without accounting definitions.
