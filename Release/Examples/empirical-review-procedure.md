## Assigning a computed status

Before inspecting the series, record the protocol version and lock date, boundary, window, units, sources, missing-data rule and any episodic exceptions. Compute the defining quantity on that basis and cite the result next to the flag. If the required evidence is missing, leave the flag unassigned and label any historical interpretation provisional. This applies wherever a Registry flag depends on a computed value.

## Worksheets for particular claims

| Use when | Record |
|---|---|
| Transferring an application quantity to another domain | Source definition and causal dependencies, destination observations, units, window and uncertainty. Mark the use *ineligible* if it conflicts with governing definitions, *needs mapping* while a dependency or measurement is unresolved, or *eligible as an application* once the mapping is complete. |
| A symbol could refer to several kinds of object | One row per use. Identify reality, selected system state, observation or agent model. Record the reporting mode (computed under a locked protocol, provisional, unimplemented or needs evidence), units and source version. |
| Making an effort claim | One row per allocation, expenditure, achieved correction or side burden. Give its units, source and reporting mode. For achieved correction identify the measured change and the matched burden. A spending record can stand alone when outcomes remain unknown. |

Use the applicable worksheet before inspecting the associated series. Record the protocol path and lock date for computed rows. These worksheet labels have procedural status.
