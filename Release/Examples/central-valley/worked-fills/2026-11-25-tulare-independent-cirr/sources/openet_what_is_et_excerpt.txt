Evapotranspiration = Evaporation + Transpiration As the sun heats the Earth’s surface, water returns to the atmosphere in two ways : 1 Evaporation: Water evaporates from the soil, open water, and other surfaces. 2 Transpiration: Water is released as vapor from plants through tiny pores in their leaves and stems called stomata. Together, these processes are called evapotranspiration or ET. Think of ET as the reverse of precipitation. Instead of water falling from the sky, ET is water rising back into the atmosphere as vapor.
