Subsidence-Affected Zones: While it is understood that most subsidence occurs below the
Corcoran Clay due to the depressurization of fine-grained sediments, it remains unclear
whether subsidence is concentrated within discrete clay lenses or occurs more broadly across
an extensive stratigraphic interval. Additional high-resolution extensometer data is needed to
determine which clay-rich zones are most susceptible to compaction and how they contribute
to overall subsidence. Improved geotechnical characterization and stratigraphic correlation
efforts could further refine the understanding of subsidence-prone zones.

Project No. 9 – Subsidence Mitigation Plan
2024 Draft GSP Description – The El Rico GSA is addressing subsidence impacts to canals
and levees that reduce conveyance and flood protection. A Subsidence Study will evaluate
causes, including groundwater pumping, using additional wells, benchmarks, and possibly
extensometers. The study will assess regional impacts and inform a mitigation plan to protect
critical infrastructure. In the interim, the GSA will respond to identified impacts, while the plan
establishes criteria and actions for any subsidence-related damage.
