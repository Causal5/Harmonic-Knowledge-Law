## What is being tested

The protocol defines \(\eta^{nat}_{TM}=R^{nat}_s/L_s\) only when a common window, a declared life stage, a natural recruitment rate, and a gross loss rate are filled. *Severed* is assigned only after that ratio is computed and equals zero (apart from declared episodes). This test asks whether currently retrievable published surveys supply those locked series for ebonyshell above and/or below Lock and Dam 19.

## Pre-registered outcome rule

A source “supplies the required series” only if it states, for a declared spatial unit (above LD 19, below LD 19, or a named pool/reach) and a declared window, both a natural recruitment rate into a declared life stage and a gross loss rate in the same units — or values from which those rates can be formed without inventing a window, a life stage, or a denominator.

| Outcome | If |
|---|---|
| **Holds** (η can be computed under the protocol) | At least one locked source supplies the required series for at least one declared unit. Then compute \(\eta\) and report it; assign *severed* only if \(\eta^{nat}_{TM}=0\) under the protocol’s episode rule. |
| **Indeterminate** (data insufficient) | No locked source supplies the required series. Leave \(\eta\) and *severed* unassigned. Qualitative “relic,” “blocked host,” or “almost disappeared” language is not a rate. |
| **Breaks** (procedure) | The fill assigns \(\eta\) or *severed* without the required series, or treats a spatial contrast or a relic hypothesis as the registry flag. |

**Cause if indeterminate.** Data gap (and possibly a scope limit of the retrieved publications). Not a Layer 0 theory failure and not a definition of A1–A8.

**Forbidden.** Do not invent recruitment or loss rates, do not treat ARM-01’s dropped spatial-contrast shortcut as a finding, and do not assign A1–A8 terms.
