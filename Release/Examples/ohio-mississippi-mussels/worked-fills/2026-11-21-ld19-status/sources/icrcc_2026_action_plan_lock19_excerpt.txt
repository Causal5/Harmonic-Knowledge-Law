The uADS has been installed in the approach channel at Lock No. 19 near
Keokuk, Iowa (Figure 16 and Figure 17) and is being operated experimentally through 2026. Both
locations are high-head dam structures with no overflow conditions and where an established
population of invasive carp exists. Available information suggests that acoustic deterrents reduce
