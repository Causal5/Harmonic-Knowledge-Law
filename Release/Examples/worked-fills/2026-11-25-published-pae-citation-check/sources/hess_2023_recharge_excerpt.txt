Site: "within the Tulare Irrigation District in the southern part of" the Central Valley (article text).

Results (pdftotext):

"Among all simulations, deep percolation increases rapidly following inundation. Fourteen days after the final inundation event, the increase in deep percolation accounts for 88.3 ± 17 % of the volume of water applied at the surface (Fig. 7a). By comparison, saturated-zone recharge efficiency is 32.7 ± 26.3 % after 2 years (Fig. 7b)."

Figure 7 caption language also states that deep percolation is "the proportion of water applied at the surface that has moved beyond the root zone within 14 d" and that saturated-zone recharge efficiency at 2 years ranges "from 0.28 % to 87.7 % (median 32.7 %)" across all 284 simulations.
