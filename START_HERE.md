# Start Here

**Current definition revision: 16 October 2026, Registry v0.3.1.**
Start with the [decision table](Release/Registry/definition-decisions-2026-10-16.md)
for the exact changes, or read this introduction for the main relationships.

## 1. Bounded knowing

Reality, a selected system state, an observation, and an agent's model are
different objects. An agent stores representations in a substrate and uses
a model to consider candidate futures. Those candidates need not exhaust
what is possible or exclude what is impossible.

Read the [registry](Release/Registry/causal-ethics-master-symbol-registry.md).

## 2. Δ-Self

The framework describes identity through a realized worldline and its
history, compared with a declared passive counterfactual. Returning to a
selected state at a later time does not repeat the earlier state-time event.
The coordinate result follows from this definition; its application to
agency needs additional evidence.

Read [Worldline](Release/Delta-Self/delta-self-worldline-formalization.md)
and [Reachability](Release/Delta-Self/delta-self-extension.md).

## 3. HKL

HKL asks whether a trajectory remains within specified dependencies and
viability constraints. Burdens retain their physical units before any
normalization. A preferred target, a passive baseline, and a failure
boundary serve different purposes. Temporary burden growth may be compatible
with viability if adequate margin remains.

Read [HKL](Release/HKL/hkl-lyapunov.md).

## 4. Absorbic effort

PAE is elective corrective effort by a capable agent. OIE is the process
through which agentic action creates or transfers destabilizing burden.
Measure resources spent separately from correction achieved. The Absorbic
Coverage Ratio compares matched correction and burden; it does not settle
ethical legitimacy or all stability questions.

Read [AEF](Release/Papers/absorbic-effort-framework.md).

## 5. Scaling and continuation

The scaling papers ask whether local knowledge, response time, resources,
and coordination can keep correction effective as systems become more
interdependent. Centralized and distributed architectures are hypotheses
to compare under specified conditions, not outcomes decided by their labels.

Read [Scaling](Release/Papers/fallacy-of-large-scale-absorbic-effort.md)
and [Continuation Filter](Release/Papers/continuation-filter.md).

## 6. Agency and representation

The Four Pillars organize proposed relationships among baseline deviation,
recursive comparison, feasible selection, and consequence tracking.
The SLP/FRF proposal addresses structured reuse and the separation of
contextual representations from admitted objective commitments.
Neither a trajectory difference nor graph curvature alone establishes cognition.

Read [Four Pillars](Release/Papers/four-pillars-of-causal-consciousness.md)
and the [SLP prototype guide](Release/slp_gdelta_harness/README.md).

## Where the work becomes measurable

The [freshwater study](Release/Examples/central-valley/README.md) starts with
stocks, flows, service, and compaction. Its updated protocol separates water
age from renewal and gross pumping from net depletion before any aggregate
HKL score is constructed. Data preparation and held-out tests are next.
