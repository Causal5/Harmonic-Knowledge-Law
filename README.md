## Document authority

Repository publication makes a document accessible; its stated status determines whether it is current, a draft, a historical source, or a review. The [v0.3 registry](Release/Registry/causal-ethics-master-symbol-registry.md) remains current. The v0.4 draft and other recovered manuscripts do not automatically supersede established papers. Cross-document consistency and empirical validation remain active work; see the [architecture policy](Release/ARCHITECTURE.md).
